## 💼 Our Capabilities

We specialize in end-to-end digital transformation, advanced analytics, and custom engineering. Our core service areas include:

*   **Custom Developments:** Tailored software engineering, enterprise web frameworks, API integrations, and bespoke applications built to solve unique business challenges.
*   **Data Engineering:** Designing and implementing robust data pipelines, data warehousing solutions, ETL processes, and automated data architectures.
*   **Machine Learning & AI:** Custom ML models, predictive analytics, AI-driven automation, and intelligent forecasting to optimize marketing efficiency.
*   **Digital Analytics:** Advanced insights powered by Adobe Analytics, Google Analytics (GA4), and Mixpanel.
*   **Marketing Automation:** Streamlining customer engagement via Adobe Campaign, Clevertap, Moengage, WebEngage, Marketo, and SFMC.
*   **Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO):** Boosting conversion efficiency using Adobe Target, Google Optimize, Optimizely, and VWO.
*   **Digital Marketing Activation:** Driving performance and brand marketing across DV360, SEO, Affiliate Marketing, Amazon DSP, and Paid Search/Social.
*   **Customer & Data Platforms:** Strategy and implementation for Adobe Experience Platform (AEP), Segment, and Data Management Platforms (Adobe Audience Manager).
*   **Training & Workshops:** Upskilling teams to master the modern MarTech stack.
